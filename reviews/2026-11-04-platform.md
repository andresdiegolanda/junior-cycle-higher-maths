# Platform review, 4 November 2026

Scope: interface, styles and scripts (source/*.js, source/*.css), build, packaging,
toolkit-PDF and test scripts (source/*.py, source/*.cjs), source/data/glossary.json,
source/data/lab-narration.json, and the build-time narration (source/spoken.py), which
since commit 4a2f4c2 generates every spoken field of the guide from the written text.
Branch review/platform, rebased onto origin/main at 2dcf58b (which merged the
narration-at-build-time change, the whole-book content review and the
context-aware narration with author-marked clock times).

How it was checked: the site was built and served locally and driven in headless
Chrome (Playwright) at 390, 768 and 1440 px in all four skins (Classic, Warm paper,
High contrast, Projector), with the operating system in light and dark mode; console
errors, horizontal overflow, tap-target sizes and text sizes were measured on eight
routes per combination; WCAG 2.2 AA contrast was computed for every visible text
node against its effective background; storage failures (localStorage throwing,
quota exceeded, corrupt and malformed records) were injected; keyboard order, focus
visibility, dialog focus and Escape were exercised; narration and guided reading were
driven with a mocked speechSynthesis; the toolkit PDF was regenerated, rendered with
pdftoppm and every text span checked to lie inside its card; two consecutive builds
were compared byte for byte; all 140 glossary entries and 86 glossary diagrams were
read, the diagrams rendered as a gallery and inspected, and the numbers recomputed;
every generated narration string of the built site (11,039 strings) was scanned for
leftover symbols, units read as letters, run-together tokens and unbalanced
brackets.

Severity key: bug, accessibility, robustness, print, glossary-error, pedagogy, typo.
