## Method

Every field of every item in scope was read. Numerical and algebraic results in
steps, skill checks, challenges, toolkit methods, cards and written practice are
recomputed with python and sympy. Every SVG in scope is parsed: coordinate-grid
diagrams (chapters 10 and 11) are decoded from their tick labels and every plotted
point and sampled line point compared with the stated equation; circle-theorem and
triangle diagrams (chapter 16) are measured for angle and radius consistency; the
pie-chart and spinner sectors (9-03, 13-02) are measured against the stated angles.
Every stored narration field is regenerated from its source text with
`source/spoken.py` and compared with the stored value; the generated narration of
every field is then scanned for unit abbreviations and symbols that `spoken()` reads
badly.

Severity key: error (mathematically wrong or wrong answer key), misleading, spec,
pedagogy, narration, typo.
