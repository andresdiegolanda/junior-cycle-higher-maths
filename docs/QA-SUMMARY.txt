Content review and Irish-convention pass, 4 November 2026
Scope
Every chapter was read in full and checked item by item: 646 teaching steps
with their diagram labels, 316 quick checks, 632 written exercises, 96 chapter
challenges, 222 toolkit methods and visual cards, the chapter reference lists
and the 140 glossary entries. Nine independent reviews (two or three chapters
each) recomputed every number, expansion, factorisation, root, ratio, angle,
probability, statistic, volume, conversion and rounding with sympy, checked
that each quick check has exactly one correct option at the stored index, that
every staged solution leads to its stated answer, and that each "check" really
verifies the answer. Explanations were judged for a 14–15-year-old working
without a teacher, and terminology was compared with Irish Junior Cycle usage.

Results
- No arithmetic or algebraic error was found in any chapter.
- 630 field-level corrections were applied: 8 conceptual errors, 263 clarity
  rewrites and 359 wording or convention changes, followed by a site-wide
  terminology pass. Six exercises that duplicated their lesson's worked example
  or a quick check were replaced with fresh items of equal difficulty
  (01-02-w2, 01-06-w1, 01-07-w1, 02-05-w1, 02-06-w2, 10-01-w2); 01-07-w4
  (polynomial remainder) now asks for an exact division and factorisation;
  15-03-w1, 16-06-w2, 21-04-w3, 21-05-w1, 22-05-w1 and 07-04-w4 were re-numbered
  so they need real working (07-04-w4 previously solved to k = 0); 22-05-w3 is
  now an angle-of-depression problem; 24-03-w4 asks about the rhombus correctly.
- Conceptual corrections: ℕ = {1, 2, 3, …} as in Irish exams (chapter 3, four
  places); the converse-of-alternate-angles proof in 08-09 cites the correct
  reason; 21-09 step 2 no longer claims that multiplying by a reciprocal
  "reverses" division; 24-03-w4 no longer contradicts its own check.
- Irish conventions throughout: slope (gradient kept as a search alias),
  factorise, right-angled triangle and perpendicular sides, interior opposite
  angles, axial and central symmetry, ⊂ for subset, #A beside n(A), mid-interval
  value, standard rate cut-off point and tax credits, F = P(1 + i)ᵗ beside the
  percentage form, 1/√3 and 1/√2 beside √3/3 and √2/2, "correct to one decimal
  place", euro and litres wording, "at random" instead of "uniformly".
  Theorem numbers (3, 4, 5, 6, 9, 12, 13, 14, 19) and corollaries are now named
  where the Junior Cycle course uses them, and chapter 8 points Higher Level
  students to the proof explanations in chapter 16. The initial claim that these
  full formal proofs were examinable was corrected in the follow-up below.
- Clarity: undefined jargon replaced (confounders, census, coverage bias,
  modulo, one-to-one, Euclidean, polynomial, radians, Δ, Σ and |…| notation in
  toolkit formulas, floor, ∝); "why" lines that restated the step or used
  senior-cycle ideas were rewritten; hints that gave the answer away and checks
  that only warned were replaced with verifications; stem-and-leaf keys are
  spelled out; coordinates in lesson text are written as (3,1) rather than
  "three comma one"; the sixteen transformation diagrams in chapter 24 now show
  axes, scale numbers, vertex coordinates, the mirror line, the centre and the
  direction of turn.
- Narration: all speech is now derived from the written content at build time
  (test-spoken.py covers 48 notations). P(x) is read as a polynomial value and
  only event brackets as probabilities; units, ratios, exam parts, index laws,
  subscripts, similarity, perpendicularity and products are spoken correctly.
- Rebuilt all 25 HTML files, the 62-page A3 PDF (adaptive pagination gave the
  longer formula rows two extra sheets) and the distribution ZIP. All automated
  checks passed: speech, guided reading, search, practice UI for 948 exercises,
  99 independent symbolic answer checks, typography, diagram marks and the
  distribution validator. A browser pass over all 25 pages at 1440 px and
  390 px opened every tab and stepped through lessons with no console errors

Limits and recommendations
The NCCA specification site was not reachable from the review environment, so
convention judgements rest on the Formulae and Tables booklet, SEC paper usage
and secondary sources; a teacher should confirm the few points flagged as
unverified in the review notes. No student pilot or audible reading audit was
done. Suggested next additions: a currency-conversion exercise (chapter 12), a
gross-pay-with-overtime lesson to support toolkit method 4 and challenge 4 in
chapter 4, an equal-width histogram example with modal class in chapter 13
(frequency density is beyond the specification), cone and prism volumes, and
construction 14 (right-angled triangle from a side and an acute angle). Inverse
functions, the discriminant and some algebraic-fraction work exceed the Junior
Cycle course and are best presented as extension material.
