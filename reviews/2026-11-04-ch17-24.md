# Review of chapters 17 to 24, ported to the narration-generating base

Date: 4 November 2026. Branch `review/ch17-24-v2`, base `cde0368` (which contains
`4a2f4c2`, build-time narration, and `e9e0f92`, the whole-book review). The original
audit was made on branch `review/ch17-24` against `c784e49`; this file restates each of
its findings with its status on the new base and adds what the port found.

Scope: chapters 17 to 24 of `source/data/chapters.json` (61 lessons, 244 steps,
122 skill checks, 84 toolkit methods, 32 challenges), the 84 cards in
`source/data/visual-cards-17-24.json`, the chapter 17 block of
`source/data/practice-14-17.txt`, all of `practice-18-20.txt`, `practice-21.txt`,
`practice-22-23.txt` and `practice-24.txt` (184 written tasks), and the chapter 17 to 24
entries of `practice-order.json` (unchanged).

## Method of the port

Every field-level change of the original branch (`git log -p c784e49..review/ch17-24`)
was compared with the same field on the new base. The stored narration fields that the
original branch regenerated (`speech`, `say`, `speechQuestion`, `speechHint`,
`speechExplanation`, `speechHints`, `speechCheck`, solution `speech`) no longer exist:
`source/build.py` now composes every narration from the written text with
`source/spoken.py`. For each such finding the generated narration of the same text was
recomputed with `spoken()` and inspected; all 1314 generated narrations in scope
(steps, checks, goals, takeaways, mistakes, methods, checks, strategies, reference rows,
challenges, cards and practice rows) were scanned for misreadings. Every number touched
was recomputed with sympy. Changes were kept to the new wording of `e9e0f92`
("factorise", "slope", "right-angled triangle", "perpendicular side", "correct to one
decimal place"); nothing of that review was reverted.

Status key: already fixed upstream (`e9e0f92` made the same or a better change),
re-applied (the fix was still needed and was applied as before), adapted (re-applied in
the new wording or in a different place), obsolete (the stored field no longer exists and
the generated narration reads correctly), deferred (a `spoken.py` rule problem, outside
this scope), disagreement (not applied, with the reason).

## Deferred to integration

1. `source/spoken.py` rule problems found by running the generated narration of every
   item in scope (exact input → wrong reading):
   - A variable h after a digit is read as hours: `C=3h+5` → "C equals 3 hours plus 5"
     (23 method 1, 20 method 5, 20-c03, 23-c02, practice 23-01-w4); `(2h−x, 2k−y)` →
     "open bracket 2 hours minus x, 2 times k minus y" (24 method 2 and card 24/2, the
     reflection in x = h, y = k).
   - `/h` is always read "per hour": `sin 30° = 6/h` → "sine 30 degrees equals 6 per
     hour" (22-05 step 1); `cos 60° = 5/h` → "5 per hour" (22-05 q1); `tan 60° = h/4`
     reads correctly.
   - Litres: bare `L` after a number is read as the letter ("240 L", "35 L": 17-04 step 2
     and q2, 17 methods 5, 6, 8, 17-c02, 17-c04); `L/min` → "L over min" (17-04 q1,
     17 method 6); `L/s` → "L over s" (17 method 7, 17-c02); `litres/s` → "litres over s"
     (17 method 7 trap).
   - `cm/s` → "cm over s" (17 method 7, 17-c02, 20-c04); `cm³/s` → "cm cubed over s"
     (17 methods 7 and 8, 17-c02, 20 method 7, 20-c04, card 17/7); `cm³/min` likewise.
   - A unit after a variable, bracket or superscript is not read: `4x m²` → "4 times x m
     squared", `x² cm²` → "x squared cm squared", `10⁶ cm³` → "10 to the power 6 cm
     cubed" (17 method 8), `π cm³` → "pi cm cubed".
   - `in²` → "in squared" (22-c02).
   - The euro rule captures a trailing comma: `€6, €18 remains` → "6, euros 18 euros"
     (21 method 5).
   - `−1 < x < 2` reads "minus 1 is less than x is less than 2"; "x is between minus 1
     and 2" would be clearer (23-04, 23 method 5).
2. Content outside scope with the same unit abbreviations (km/h in chapter 12 now reads
   correctly; L in chapter 6 still does not).
3. One ⟂ (U+27C2) remains in chapter 11 while the rest of the guide uses ⊥.
4. Chapter 23 graph tick labels (and those of chapters 1 and 11) write negative numbers
   with a hyphen; a change in the graph generator would fix all of them at once.
5. Thin spec coverage in scope that would need new material: a cylinder net task
   (GT.2(b)) and an explicit f:x ↦ example (AF.7(b)); the glossary "Function" entry could
   add the f:x ↦ notation.
6. SEC material (Formulae and Tables booklet, exam archive) was not fetched; the sphere
   formula form in the booklet remains UNVERIFIED.
7. No test hard-codes a count that this port changed; all checks pass unchanged.
