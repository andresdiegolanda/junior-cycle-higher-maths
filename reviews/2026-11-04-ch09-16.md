## Method

Every field of every item in scope was read against the `cde0368` text (the
semantic diff from `c784e49` was reviewed first so that nothing already corrected
was redone). Every numerical and algebraic result in steps, skill checks,
challenges, toolkit methods, cards and written practice was recomputed by hand and,
where non-trivial, with python (probabilities, Venn regions, simultaneous
solutions, slopes, distances, intersections, durations, speeds, pie angles,
densities, means, indices, surds, standard form, discriminants, roots, similar
triangle scales, circle angles). Every coordinate-grid SVG in chapters 10 and 11
was decoded from its tick labels and every plotted point and sampled line point
compared with the stated equation; the chapter 16 circle and triangle diagrams were
measured for radius, angle and right-angle consistency; the chapter 9 spinner and
chapter 13 pie sectors were measured against the stated angles. Since narration is
now generated at build time, `spoken()` was run on every narrated field in scope and
the output scanned for symbols, units and bracket forms it reads badly; those caused
by the written text were fixed in the text, those caused by `spoken.py` rules are
listed under "Deferred to integration" with the exact input and reading.

Severity key: error (mathematically wrong or wrong answer key), misleading, spec,
pedagogy, narration, typo.
