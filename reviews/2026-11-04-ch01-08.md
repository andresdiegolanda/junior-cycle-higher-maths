# Review of chapters 1 to 8

Date: 4 November 2026. Branch `review/ch01-08`, base `cde0368` (merge of the whole-book
review, PR #19; narration is generated at build time from the written text).

Scope: chapters 1 to 8 of `source/data/chapters.json` (Algebra 1, Factors, Sets, Applied
Arithmetic, Statistics 1, Perimeter/Area/Volume, Statistics 2, Geometry 1: 53 lessons, 221
steps, 106 skill checks, 71 toolkit methods, 32 challenges, 50 reference entries), the 71
cards in `source/data/visual-cards-01-08.json`, and the written practice rows for these
chapters in `source/data/practice-01-04.txt` and `practice-05-08.txt` (212 tasks).

## Method

Every field of every item in scope was read. Every numerical and algebraic result in steps,
skill checks, challenges, toolkit examples, cards and written practice was recomputed with
python and sympy (expansions, factorisations, quotients and remainders, substitutions,
inequalities, Venn-region counts and bounds, percentage, tax and compound-interest chains,
currency conversions, areas, volumes, scale conversions, means, medians, ranges, missing
values, angle chains and Pythagorean lengths). Every step SVG was parsed: text labels were
compared with the step text, and the polygon coordinates of the chapter 6 and 8 diagrams
were measured for side ratios, equal sides, angles and midpoints. Narration was checked by
running `source/spoken.py` on the built `compiled-data.json` for chapters 1 to 8 and
scanning the result for mis-read symbols, units, currency and arrows.

Severity key: error (mathematically wrong or wrong answer key), misleading, spec, pedagogy,
narration, typo.

## Deferred to integration

All of the following are rule-level problems in `source/spoken.py` (owned by the interface
reviewer) or content outside this scope. Each gives the exact input and the current reading.

1. Cardinality `n(A)` is read as a product: `n(A) = 22; n(B) = 18` becomes "n times open
   bracket A close bracket equals 22; n times open bracket B close bracket equals 18", and
   `n(A ∩ B) = 5` becomes "n times open bracket A intersection B close bracket equals 5".
   The `([a-z])\(` "times" rule fires before any cardinality rule. There are 54 occurrences
   in chapters 1 to 8 (lessons 03-01, 03-04, 03-05, the chapter 3 reference, toolkit and
   challenges, 03-c01). Suggested rule, placed before the "times" rules:
   `t = re.sub(r'\bn\(([^()]+)\)', r'the number of elements in \1', t)` (the same wording the
   `#` rule already uses).
2. A euro amount followed by a comma swallows the comma: `23% of €80, so subtracting`
   becomes "23 percent of 80, euros so subtracting"; `€25,000, €27,000` would become
   "25,000, euros 27,000, euros". The regex `€\s*([\d,]+(?:\.\d+)?)` should be
   `€\s*(\d+(?:,\d{3})*(?:\.\d+)?)` (and the same for £ and $). Seventeen places in the
   chapter 1 to 8 text and five practice rows (04-01-w1, w3, w4, 04-05-w4, 04-06-w1) are
   affected.
3. `€1` is read "1 euros" (`Suppose the example rate is €1 = £0.85` becomes "1 euros equals
   0.85 pounds"; also 04-05 q1 and q2, 04-05-w1, w2, the chapter 4 toolkit and card 4/6).
   In Irish usage "euro" is both singular and plural; "1 euro" or simply "euro" would read
   better.
4. Units after π or a closing bracket are left as abbreviations: `C = 2π×6 = 12π cm` is
   read "12 pi cm", `A = π×6² = 36π cm²` "36 pi cm squared", `(14 + 7π) cm` "close bracket
   cm", `(80 + 20π) m` "close bracket m" (06-03 steps 2 to 4 and q1, 06-c02, practice
   06-03-w1 to w4). The unit rule requires a digit immediately before the unit.
5. Multi-letter monomials: `12x²y + 18xy²` is read "12 times x squared y plus 18xy squared"
   and `HCF = 6xy` "HCF equals 6xy" (02-01 steps 1 to 4, card 2/0 label). The "times" rule
   `(\d)([A-Za-rt-z])\b` needs a word boundary after one letter.
6. The complement `A′` is read "A prime" (03-02 step 3, 03-02 q2, chapter 3 reference and
   toolkit, practice 03-02-w2, 03-03-w3). Classrooms say "A complement" or "A dash"; a
   set-context rule (′ after a capital letter not followed by a coordinate) could read
   "complement".
7. `segment [AB]` (08-01 step 1) keeps the square brackets in the spoken text.
8. Arrows in implication statements are read "gives" (`Parallel lines → equal corresponding
   angles` becomes "Parallel lines gives equal corresponding angles"; 08-01, 08-04, 08-06,
   08-08, 08-09, 08-12). Equation chains read acceptably; a wording such as "leads to" or
   "so" would suit both.
9. Practice rows: `$1.08 (US dollars)` now reads "1.08 dollars open bracket US dollars close
   bracket"; a `US$` rule would let the original notation be restored.
10. No test hard-codes a count that this review changed; all checks pass unchanged.
11. Blocked sources: the curriculumonline.ie landing page and examinations.ie (Formulae and
    Tables booklet, exam archive) could not be fetched; the specification PDF and Appendix B
    were downloaded directly. Booklet-related claims are marked UNVERIFIED above.
12. Thin coverage needing new material rather than a fix: a prism-net drawing task
    (GT.2(b)) and a worked step for a single term times a trinomial (AF.3(b)(I)).
